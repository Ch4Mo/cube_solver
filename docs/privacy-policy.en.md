# Cube Solver privacy policy

*Last updated: 12 November 2026*

Cube Solver ("the app") is a free app that helps you solve the 3×3 cube,
published by Ch4Mo ("we"). This page explains which data is used, why, and
what your choices are.

## 1. Camera and photos of the cube

The app uses the camera only to photograph the faces of your cube. Photos are
**analysed directly on your phone** to detect the colours, then immediately
discarded:

- they are **not saved** to your gallery or anywhere else;
- they are **not sent** over the Internet, to us or to anyone else.

You can refuse camera access and enter the colours by hand.

## 2. No account, no personal data collected by us

The app does not ask for an account, a name or an email address. We do not
collect, store or sell any personal data.

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is funded by advertising provided by **Google AdMob**. To show ads,
measure their performance and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may
automatically collect some data from your device, including:

- the device advertising ID (Android) or the IDFA (iOS, only if you allow it);
- the IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
- information about the device and the app (model, system, language);
- interactions with ads and diagnostic data.

This data is processed by Google under its own rules:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

**Your consent.** If you are in the European Economic Area, the United
Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form is shown on first launch: you can
accept or refuse personalised ads. You can change this choice at any time in
the app: "About" button (ⓘ) on the home screen, then "Ad preferences". If you
refuse, non-personalised ads may still be shown.

You can also reset or disable the advertising ID in your phone settings.

## 4. Children

The app is not specifically directed at children under 13 and does not
knowingly collect any data about them.

## 5. Security

Exchanges with Google's advertising services are encrypted (HTTPS).

## 6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase and object.
Since we hold no data about you, in practice these requests concern the
advertising data processed by Google, which you can manage from your Google
account or your device settings.

## 7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of the page shows the last
change.

## 8. Contact
