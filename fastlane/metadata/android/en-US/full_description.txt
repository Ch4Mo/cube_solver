Stuck on your 3×3 cube? Cube Solver solves it with you.

📷 SCAN
Photograph the 6 faces with the camera. A grid helps you frame each face, a hint tells you how to hold the cube, and the light can be switched on if the room is dark.

✅ CHECK
Colours are detected automatically, even in difficult lighting. Uncertain squares are highlighted and can be corrected with a single tap. You can also enter all the colours by hand.

🧩 SOLVE
The app computes a short solution (about 20 moves) and guides you step by step: a 3D cube with an arrow shows each move, along with a clear explanation ("Turn the right face a quarter turn clockwise").

🔍 ERROR DETECTION
• Misread colour or missing square
• Corner twisted in place, flipped edge or swapped pieces: the app tells you the cube has been taken apart or tampered with and cannot be solved as it is.
• While solving, a photo of the front face is enough to check you are on track: the app spots a missed or extra move and helps you get back on track.

🌍 Available in English, French and Spanish.

🔒 PRIVACY
Photos are analysed directly on your phone: they are never sent or saved.

The app is free and supported by ads.
